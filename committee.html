OUR COMMITMENT

Ready to Welcome the World

The Organising Committee is committed to delivering an inclusive, professionally organised and memorable International Conference of Physics Students in New Delhi. We look forward to welcoming students from across the world to celebrate science, collaboration and friendship at ICPS 2028.

"Together, we will shape an unforgettable ICPS."

From scientific excellence to cultural exchange, our team is working to ensure that every participant experiences the very best of India and the global physics community.
