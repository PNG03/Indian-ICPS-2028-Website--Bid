Social Programmes

EXPERIENCE INDIA

More Than A Conference

ICPS has always been about more than scientific discussions. It is about building friendships, experiencing new cultures and creating memories that last a lifetime. Our social programme has been designed to encourage interaction, celebrate diversity and showcase the rich cultural heritage of India.

SOCIAL PROGRAMMES

Social Events

IAPS Saga of Discoveries

An evening celebrating the scientific heritage of nations through short performances inspired by famous scientists, discoveries and ideas.

Nations Evening

Delegates share their traditions, music, cuisine and culture while celebrating the diversity that makes ICPS unique.

Quantum Quest

A multi-day puzzle challenge where teams solve interconnected scientific and logical problems throughout the conference.

Indian Cultural Night

Traditional performances, dance, music and cuisine showcasing India's incredible cultural diversity.

CULTURAL EXPERIENCES

Discover India Together

Regional Cuisine

Taste authentic flavours from different parts of India during the conference.

Classical Arts & Folk Traditions

Experience India's rich artistic heritage through classical music, folk and classical dances celebrating remarkable cultural diversity.

Heritage Walks

Experience Delhi's historic monuments and vibrant streets.

Networking Evenings

Relaxed social gatherings designed to help participants connect beyond scientific sessions.

EXCURSIONS

Explore the Heart of India

Old & New Delhi

Discover Delhi's remarkable blend of ancient heritage and modern architecture, from centuries-old monuments to vibrant markets and cultural landmarks.

Agra Excursion

Visit the world-famous Taj Mahal and other Mughal architectural masterpieces, offering an unforgettable cultural experience.

Jaipur Experience

Explore the Pink City through its royal palaces, historic forts and colourful streets, showcasing Rajasthan's unique heritage.

Local Experiences

Optional visits to museums, gardens, science centres and traditional markets throughout the conference week.

CONFERENCE DINNER

An Evening to Remember

The Conference Dinner will celebrate the friendships, collaborations and memories created during ICPS 2028. Participants will enjoy Indian cuisine, cultural performances and an evening of celebration with the international physics community.

The evening will also feature recognition of volunteers, student presentations and memorable moments from throughout the conference.

Celebration

A relaxed evening with delegates from across the globe.

Awards

Recognition for outstanding presentations and activities.

UNTIL WE MEET AGAIN

Closing Ceremony

The conference concludes with a celebration of everything achieved throughout the week. New friendships, scientific collaborations and unforgettable memories will accompany every participant long after ICPS 2028 comes to an end.

"Physics connects minds. ICPS connects people."

We hope every participant leaves India inspired, motivated and eager to meet again at future International Conferences of Physics Students.
