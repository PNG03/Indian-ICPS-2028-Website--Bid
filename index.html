WHY INDIA?

A Destination Where Science Meets Civilization

India has long been a meeting point of ideas, cultures, discoveries and innovation. From ancient mathematical breakthroughs to world-class research institutions, scientific curiosity has remained central to its identity.

Today, India hosts globally recognised universities, cutting-edge research centres, a thriving student community and internationally connected infrastructure, making it an ideal destination for the International Conference of Physics Students.

ICPS 2028 aims to celebrate not only physics, but also collaboration, diversity and friendship by bringing together young physicists from around the world in an environment designed for learning, exploration and unforgettable cultural exchange.

Scientific Excellence

Home to leading research institutes, laboratories and internationally recognised universities.

Global Connectivity

Excellent international air connectivity, modern infrastructure and welcoming hospitality.

Student Community

One of the world's largest and most active physics student communities.

Culture & Heritage

Thousands of years of history combined with vibrant modern cities and unforgettable experiences.

WHY DELHI?

India's Scientific Capital

As India's capital, New Delhi offers an exceptional blend of scientific institutions, international connectivity, cultural heritage and modern infrastructure. Home to premier universities and research centres, it provides an inspiring environment for meaningful scientific exchange.

With excellent accommodation, world-class conference venues, efficient transport and easy access to iconic historical landmarks, Delhi offers participants a conference experience that extends far beyond lecture halls.
