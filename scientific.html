Scientific Programmes

SCIENTIFIC PROGRAMMES

A Week Dedicated to Physics

The scientific programmes of ICPS 2028 have been designed to encourage learning, discussion and collaboration among physics students from across the world. Through lectures, workshops, student presentations and poster sessions, participants will explore a broad spectrum of modern physics while exchanging ideas with peers and experts.

WORKSHOPS & TUTORIALS

Learn Beyond the Classroom

Interactive workshops will introduce participants to emerging computational techniques, modern experimental methods and research tools used across contemporary physics. Sessions are designed to encourage active participation rather than passive learning.

Tutorials will be conducted by experienced researchers and invited experts, providing practical exposure to techniques that students can apply in their own research.

Computational Physics

Scientific computing, simulations and data analysis.

Machine Learning

AI applications in modern physics research.

Experimental Techniques

Instrumentation, measurements and laboratories.

Research Skills

Scientific writing, communication and publishing.

TECHNICAL VISITS

Experience Science in Action

Participants will have the opportunity to visit leading research laboratories and scientific institutions in and around New Delhi, providing first-hand exposure to cutting-edge research facilities, advanced instrumentation and ongoing scientific projects.
