Venue & Accommodation

CONFERENCE VENUE

A World-Class Setting for ICPS 2028

The successful hosting of the G20 Leaders' Summit 2023 placed Bharat Mandapam among the world's distinguished convention venues. The 123.5-acre site of Bharat Mandapam at Lutyens' Delhi close to Purana Qila has become India's major venue for international meetings, incentives, conferences, and exhibitions (MICES). The MICE sector in this context signifies the spread of knowledge and the joint effort to find solutions. By drawing from the architecture of the Shankha (conch shell), the building merges modern engineering with traditional Indian design through the use of landscaped water features, circular layouts, and cultural motifs.

A major highlight of the venue, the entrance is flanked by the 8.8-meter tall Ashtadhatu bronze statue of Nataraja which is the largest in this form. The statue was crafted by internationally acclaimed sculptor Radhakrishnan Shastri through the traditional method of wax-resin casting.

ACCOMMODATION

Comfortable Stay for Every Delegate

The Lalit is centrally situated, close to the city's main commercial and cultural area - Connaught Place. Connaught Place being a central hub for the city’s vibrant life makes The Lalit an ideal accommodation venue to host ICPS 2028. Well equipped with 457 rooms, the 5-star hotel has been thoughtfully decorated throughout to create an atmosphere of peace and elegance. Apart from that, it is located at a convenient distance of 24 km from the Indira Gandhi International airport which is the main connecting point to the city for international travelers. Also, with its prime position, The Lalit assures quick reach to the city's iconic venues like India Gate, Red Fort, Jama Masjid, as well as city centers and shopping zones without being far away.

Also worthy of consideration are the facilities provided by The Lalit– one can enjoy a wide choice of cuisine from the hotel dining spots, with open access to fitness and wellness options and the open spaces as well. They also have an art collection which takes up a significant space of their public area. Considering its extensive record of hosting many high-profile and international diplomatic conferences and meetings, you can be assured of a very good service experience here.
